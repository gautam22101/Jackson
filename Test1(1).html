About Jackson Chemical Industries

Three Decades of Chemical Excellence

Established in 1992, Jackson Chemical Industries is a leading manufacturer of dye intermediates and specialty chemicals based in Ankleshwar, Gujarat, India. With over three decades of experience, we have built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and innovation in the chemical manufacturing industry.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in the GIDC Industrial Estate, Ankleshwar, is equipped with modern reactors and processing equipment capable of handling a wide range of chemical synthesis processes including sulfonation, nitration, reduction, oxidation, and condensation reactions.

We serve a diverse clientele across the globe, supplying high-quality intermediates for acid dyes, direct dyes, reactive dyes, and disperse dyes. Our commitment to quality, competitive pricing, and timely delivery has made us a preferred supplier for dye manufacturers worldwide.
